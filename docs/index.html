CSC OCT Analyser

Central serous chorioretinopathy detection on OCT B-scans with five fused CNNs, selectable screening and balanced operating points, and referral of uncertain scans to a clinician. The study behind it also checks whether high scores come from the disease or from how the images were collected.

Sai Sathya Krishna A R, Karthik B, Adharsh Ramakrishnan, Dr. Swaminathan A · VIT Chennai

Limitations

  • Five CSC patients in the same-scanner data, so intervals are wide.
  • Models do not transfer to other scanners in cross-dataset tests (AUC 0.435 and 0.600).
  • Acute versus chronic subtyping is experimental and not reliable.
